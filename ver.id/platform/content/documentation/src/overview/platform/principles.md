# Principles

## Our mission

Our mission is to equip developers with the right tools to integrate decentralized identity apps into their online platforms. We aim to restore digital autonomy and ensure that privacy and security are foundational principles governing every interaction in the digital realm by helping the paradigm shift on the internet towards a more decentralized internet. We are committed to developing robust, user-friendly tools and technologies that meet the current and future needs of developers, enabling platforms to accept decentralized identities with ease and paving the way for a more secure, private, and user-centric internet.

## Our vision

We envision a future where online identity is redefined through the revolutionary potential of decentralized technology, creating a digital landscape where every individual has unassailable control over their personal data. Our vision is to establish a new paradigm of digital sovereignty, where personal information is no longer the currency of the digital age but a sacred trust managed by each individual. In this future, privacy is safeguarded, trust is paramount, and the internet evolves into a realm where user agency, respect, and security prevail. Through our efforts, we aim to catalyze a digital renaissance, ushering in an era of digital freedom and sovereignty where users and platforms alike are empowered to interact with confidence and autonomy.

Together, let's revolutionize online identity and explore a new era of digital freedom and sovereignty.
