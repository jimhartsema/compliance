# Client tiers

We have four basic tiers in our platform. At the moment we only accept clients in the Professional or higher tier due to the nature of our starting business.

## Tiers

- **Playground**: This tier is for development and testing purposes only. Clients typically start in this tier to understand our platform, the underlying technology, and start testing in their own development environment. All products are available, however, only very limited in terms of identity apps, attributes, and number of requests are allowed.
- **Basic**: This thier is for straight-forward, simply use-cases only (such as verification of 18+). Clients in this tier have limited opportunities for customization in terms of identity apps, domains, flows and required attributes.
- **Professional**: This tier is for the larger, professional clients. Clients in this tier are typically larger organisations with in-house or mature development expertise, combined with a mature internal control environment and compliance functions such as a privacy officer. In this tier, a high-level of customization in terms of number of identity apps, flows, attributes are available in high volume.
- **Enterprise**: This tier is for the largest group of clients, who require a high-level of customization or embedding in for instance their onboarding flow. All functionalities, all identity apps, all attributes in the highest level of customization, access and volume are available.
