# Login

Decentralized identity apps redefine the login experience, shifting from traditional, password-based methods to a secure, passwordless system utilizing QR codes. This modern approach enhances security, simplifies the login process, and significantly improves user experience by leveraging user-controlled, decentralized authentication.

## Key Benefits

**User Control**: Offer your customers unparalleled control over their login process. Passwordless authentication ensures a seamless, secure access method, reinforcing user autonomy over digital identities.

**Enhanced User Experience**: Transform the login journey with a quick QR code scan, facilitating a smoother, more intuitive user interface.

**Elimination of Password Risks**: Passwordless login eradicates common vulnerabilities linked to password authentication, including the dangers of password reuse and susceptibility to brute-force attacks.

**Inherent Multi-Factor Security**: Utilize decentralized identity apps as a robust authentication framework, potentially removing the need for traditional two-factor authentication methods that rely on less secure channels like SMS or email.

**Regulatory Compliance**: Meet and exceed compliance requirements with GDPR and other standards by minimizing the collection and storage of personal data through passwordless authentication.

## How it works

**Initiation**: Customers initiate the login process by accessing the login page on your platform.

**Method**: They’re redirected to our platform to choose their identity app, which generates a QR code.

**Verification**: Scanning the QR code initiates the authentication, with the identity app verifying the customer’s credentials.

**Completion**: Authentication concludes with your platform processing the credentials, granting the customer access.
