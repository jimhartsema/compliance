# Signing

Decentralized identity revolutionizes the concept of electronic signatures by integrating them with government-verified credentials, significantly elevating their compliance, security, and authenticity. Traditional drawn signatures are replaced with digital signatures that are cryptographically secure and verifiable, ensuring the integrity and legal trustworthiness of digital documents and transactions.

## Key Benefits

**Regulatory Compliance**: Leverage government-verified credentials through decentralized identity to meet and exceed regulatory and industry-specific standards for electronic signatures.

**Enhanced Security**: By replacing traditional drawn signatures with cryptographically secure, verifiable digital signatures, decentralized identity enhances the security and trustworthiness of electronic signatures.

**User Control**: Empower users with full control over their data. Through selective disclosure, they share only necessary information, aligning with data minimization principles.

**Authenticity Guarantee**: Ensure the authenticity and legal trustworthiness of digital signatures, providing a solid foundation for digital agreements and transactions.

**Operational Efficiency**: Streamline your digital signature processes, reducing turnaround times and operational costs, thereby enhancing overall workflow efficiency.

## How it works

**Initiation**: The process starts when your customer clicks a designated button on your platform.

**Method**: They’re redirected to our platform to choose their identity app, which generates a QR code.

**Verification**: Scanning the QR code with their identity app initiates the signature process.

**Completion**: The process concludes once your platform has processed and verified the signature outcomes.
