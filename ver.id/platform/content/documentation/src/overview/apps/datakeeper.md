## Description

[Datakeeper](https://www.datakeeper.nl/en) is a comprehensive identity app initially developed by Rabobank but now fully standalone. It houses an extensive range of financial data points predominantly used in the financial services sector, covering mortgages, rentals, and private leases. The app facilitates a swift, easy, and secure method for customers to share their personal data. Given its versatile application in finance, incorporating Datakeeper into our platform would significantly enhance our offerings for clients operating in these industries.
