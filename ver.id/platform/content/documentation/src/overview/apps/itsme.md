## Description

[itsme®](https://www.itsme-id.com/en-NL) is a digital ID service in Belgium that offers a secure and easy way to log in, create accounts, confirm transactions, and sign documents electronically with its simple app. It eliminates the need for passwords or card readers and is recognized legally like a handwritten signature. itsme® is used by millions in Belgium and is available at over 800 companies and platforms. It combines ease of use with high digital security and privacy, ensuring that all actions are user-approved and transparent. It is used in a wide variety of use-cases with predominantely PID data.
