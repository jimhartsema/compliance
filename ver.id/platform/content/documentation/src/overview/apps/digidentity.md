## Description

[Digidentity](https://www.digidentity.eu/) is founded in the Netherlands in 2008 and has a large userbase mainly in the e-Herkenning space. It offers services such as identity proofing, authentication, and electronic signatures. Trusted by governments and businesses worldwide, Digidentity ensures high security levels, supporting over 25 million verified identities. It provides customizable solutions for businesses and developers, helping integrate digital identity systems into existing workflows, which include capabilities like KYC, AML, and remote employee onboarding.
