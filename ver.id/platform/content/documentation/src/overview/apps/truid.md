## Description

[Truid](https://truid.app) is a digital identity and access management platform based in Sweden. It provides a comprehensive solution for secure online identity verification, emphasizing user privacy and security. The platform uses biometric verification, document verification, and multi-factor authentication to ensure high levels of data protection. Truid supports a variety of applications across industries, enabling users to control their digital identities and manage consent. The service is designed to comply with global data compliance standards, including GDPR.

Although originally based in Sweden, it is used more often in Eastern-Europe. The app is capable of importing Personal Identifiable Data (PID) data from approx 6400 different ID-docs (Passports, ID-cards) globally, making it an ideal solution to customers around the globe.
