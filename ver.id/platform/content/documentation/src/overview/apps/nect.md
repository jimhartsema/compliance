## Description

[Nect](https://nect.com/en/) is a German based identity app, based in Hamburg. It is originated in the Insurance Industry, and has a wide variety of use-cases in automotive, finance and government sector. They specialize in digital identity solutions, offering products such as Nect Ident, Nect Sign, and eID Function. Their technology focuses on a fully automated online identification process that can be completed in just a few minutes using an app. This process involves capturing an ID document and a selfie video for verification. Nect aims to provide a user-friendly and secure identification experience for all its users.
