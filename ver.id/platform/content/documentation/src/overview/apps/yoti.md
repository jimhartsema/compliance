## Description

[Yoti](https://yoti.com) is a platform that provides digital identity services to users primarily in the UK. It was launched with the aim of making it easier and safer for individuals to prove their identities online and in person. Yoti offers a free app where users can create a digital ID by securely storing personal details and sharing only necessary information when required. The app uses face scans to verify identities, which helps in protecting users against impersonation.

Yoti also focuses on social impact, being certified as a B Corp and actively engaging in various social purpose initiatives. They work with various industries including financial services, healthcare, government, and retail, providing solutions like age verification, identity verification, eSigning, and authentication.
