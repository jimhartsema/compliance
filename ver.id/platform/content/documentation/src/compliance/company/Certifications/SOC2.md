# Service Organization Control 2

SOC 2, developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA), is a widely recognized standard for assessing the security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy of information for cloud-based service providers. Adhering to SOC 2 standards means that we will establish and implement rigorous controls and processes to protect against unauthorized access, maintain system availability, and uphold the confidentiality and integrity of sensitive information on top of our existing compliance measures.

## Status

We are currently in the implementation phase. This phase focuses on updating and formalizing our policies, procedures, and controls to address any identified gaps. Once implementation is complete, an internal audit will be conducted to assess our alignment with the SOC 2 trust service criteria in preparation for certification as of Q1 2026.
