# NEN 7510

NEN 7510 is a Dutch standard specifically focused on information security within the healthcare sector, providing guidelines and requirements for ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive patient data. In the context of Ver.iD, adherence to NEN 7510 is paramount as it underscores our commitment to safeguarding personal health information entrusted to us by healthcare providers and patients alike. By complying with NEN 7510 standards, we ensure that our platform maintains the highest standards of security, protecting sensitive healthcare data from unauthorized access, tampering, or breaches. This not only enhances trust among stakeholders but also demonstrates our dedication to upholding the integrity of healthcare information systems and the privacy of individuals' medical records.
