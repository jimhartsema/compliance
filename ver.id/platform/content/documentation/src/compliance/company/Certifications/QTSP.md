# Qualified Trust Service Provider (QTSP)

Under the [eIDAS2.0 legislation](../../regulations/eidas-2.md), Qualified Trust Service Providers (QTSPs) play an important role in the digital identity ecosystem in the European Union (EU). QTSPs are entities recognized by EU member states to provide trust services within the eIDAS2.0 ecosystem. The trust services are listed in the table below.

Ver.iD plays an enabling role in the eIDAS 2.0 ecosystem by serving as the bridge between issuers, wallets, and relying parties. Our platform supports the issuance of (Qualified) Electronic Attestation of Attributes ((Q)EAA) and Person Identification Data (PID), ensuring they meet eIDAS 2.0 and relevant ETSI standards. On the other side, we facilitate the authentication and verification of (Q)EAA and PID, enabling seamless and trustworthy interactions between EUDI Wallets, commercial wallets, and relying parties across borders. Through this, Ver.iD contributes to a secure, interoperable, and privacy-respecting European digital identity framework.

## Status

The ETSI standard compliance project at Ver.iD is progressing according to plan. Following the assessment, planning, and discovery phases throughout 2024 and early 2025, we completed a thorough gap analysis in Q2 2025. We are now in the implementation phase (Q3 2025), where updated security policies, procedures, and controls are being put in place to close identified gaps. This will be followed by continuous monitoring of updates in the relevant ETSI standards, with an external audit scheduled for Q3 2026 for QTSP certification.
