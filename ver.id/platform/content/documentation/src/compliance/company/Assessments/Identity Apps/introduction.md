# Identity app assessment

In our commitment to ensuring a trustworthy decentralized identity ecosystem, every identity app on our platform undergoes a rigorous assessment before it is deployed. These apps often manage highly sensitive information, such as social security numbers, gender, and other personal attributes. Given the critical nature of this data, it is essential that each app is secure, operates on a sound business model, and provides valuable data attributes to our users.

To this end, our comprehensive evaluation process must be completed before an identity app is integrated into our production environment. This ensures that all apps made available to our clients through our dashboard are not only functional but also adhere to the highest standards of safety and data privacy.
