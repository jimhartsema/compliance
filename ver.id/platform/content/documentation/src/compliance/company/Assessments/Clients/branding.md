# Branding

Clients may upload branding logos to our platform that can be used in [Authentication](../../../overview//platform/use-cases/authentication.md), [Onboarding](../../../overview//platform/use-cases/onboarding.md) or [Signing](../../../overview//platform/use-cases/signing.md) flows facing their customers. It is paramount that the branding and logos in the flows correspond to the correct relying party in order to instill trust and be compliant to GDPR regulations. In our dashboard, these branding and logos are self-serviced and managed.

## Reviews

We perform a review, automatically enforced, prior to a brand and/or logo can be applied in any flow. A client can have a 'default' logo and brand. As well as multiple brands or logos, for the larger type of organizations. The number of brands and logos are restricted based on the client tier.
